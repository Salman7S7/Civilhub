\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

Urban development and residential construction in Bangladesh are heavily regulated by complex building codes and municipal bylaws, such as the Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC 2020) and development authorities including RAJUK, CDA, RDA, and KDA. However, prospective land owners, real estate developers, and civil engineering practitioners frequently struggle with opaque setback rules, Floor Area Ratio (FAR) calculations, unpredictable construction cost overruns, and fragmented communication with verified engineering professionals.

This report presents \textbf{CivilHub}, a comprehensive, full-stack civil engineering, architectural planning, and regulatory intelligence platform tailored specifically for the construction sector in Bangladesh. CivilHub integrates an automated regulatory feasibility engine, a 5-step interactive construction cost estimator, an architectural floor plan catalog, a land transfer tax and mutation calculator, and a dual-mode consultation workspace powered by Google Gemini generative AI and verified human structural, architectural, and geotechnical engineers. Built on a modular React Native/Expo client, a Node.js/Express REST backend, and a relational MySQL database, CivilHub demonstrates the practical application of software engineering principles to bridge the gap between complex building regulations and accessible digital tooling.

\section{Introduction}
Civil engineering and architectural planning are foundational to safe, sustainable, and orderly urban development. In developing countries such as Bangladesh, rapid urbanization has spurred immense private and commercial construction activity. However, navigating statutory building codes, calculating permissible floor areas, estimating multi-trade material and labor costs, and obtaining authentic professional advice remain daunting challenges for ordinary land owners and developers. Traditional workflows rely heavily on informal, manual consultations and static spreadsheets, frequently resulting in building rule violations, budget deficits, and structural safety compromises.

With the proliferation of modern web and mobile platforms and advances in generative artificial intelligence, there is a compelling opportunity to digitize and democratize architectural and structural engineering knowledge. \textbf{CivilHub} was conceived and developed as a centralized digital platform that consolidates building code validation, architectural exploration, itemized cost estimation, land duty calculations, and expert consultation into a unified, user-friendly environment.

The system is designed in adherence to core software engineering principles, emphasizing modularity, component reusability, transactional data consistency, secure role-based access control, and cross-platform compatibility across mobile and web environments.

\section{Motivation}
The development of CivilHub is motivated by several acute challenges prevalent in Bangladesh's construction and building management ecosystem:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Regulatory Complexity and Code Non-Compliance:} The Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC 2020) along with specific rules from regional development authorities (e.g., RAJUK Imarat Nirman Bidhimala, CDA, RDA, KDA) dictate strict limits on Maximum Ground Coverage (MGC), Floor Area Ratio (FAR), mandatory front/rear/side setbacks, and road frontage widths. Most land owners lack the technical literacy to interpret these rules, leading to unauthorized construction, administrative fines, or demolition notices.
    \item \textbf{Cost Uncertainty and Budget Overruns:} Building construction involves interdependent expenditures across structural RCC work, interior/exterior finishes, electrical fittings, and sanitary plumbing. Land owners often receive vague lump-sum contractor quotes without transparent unit-rate breakdowns, causing severe financial strain during execution.
    \item \textbf{Scarcity of Accessible Expert Consultation:} Connecting with licensed professional engineers (IAB-registered Architects, PEng/IEB Structural Engineers, Geotechnical Specialists) is often geographically constrained and expensive. A digital bridge that facilitates direct client-engineer consultation is urgently needed.
    \item \textbf{Absence of Jurisdiction-Aware Engineering AI Tools:} Generic conversational AI models lack contextual ground-truth knowledge regarding Bangladesh's municipal zoning laws and local unit systems (Katha, Bigha, Decimal, Square Feet, BDT currency). Developing an AI assistant explicitly grounded in BNBC 2020 and local municipal bylaws provides invaluable preliminary guidance.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Objectives of the System}
The overarching objective of the CivilHub project is to design, implement, test, and document an intuitive, reliable, and secure platform that empowers citizens, builders, and engineers in Bangladesh with accessible building planning tools.

The specific technical and functional objectives are as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item To implement an automated \textbf{Feasibility Checker} that evaluates plot dimensions, road frontage, and target stories against BNBC 2020, RAJUK, CDA, RDA, and KDA regulatory requirements.
    \item To build a \textbf{Smart Architectural Designs Catalog} featuring residential and commercial layouts with 3D elevations, floor plan specifications, and cross-module synchronization.
    \item To engineer a \textbf{5-Step Construction Cost Estimator} that dynamically computes itemized structural, finishing, electrical, and plumbing costs in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT) based on quality grades (Standard, Premium, Luxury).
    \item To provide a \textbf{Land Tax and Transfer Duty Calculator} reflecting current Bangladesh stamp duty, registration fees, local government taxes, gain taxes, and mutation costs.
    \item To develop a \textbf{Dual-Mode Expert Consultation System} integrating Google Gemini AI for instant regulatory answers alongside a dedicated Messenger-style workspace connecting clients with verified licensed engineers.
    \item To enforce robust \textbf{Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)} and stateless JWT authentication separating Client and Engineer disciplines (Architect, Structural Engineer, Geotechnical Specialist).
    \item To adhere to software engineering rigor across architecture design, database normalization, RESTful API development, and validation testing.
\end{itemize}

\section{System Overview}
CivilHub is structured around a multi-tier, client-server software architecture designed to deliver high responsiveness and maintainability across web and mobile viewports:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Client Tier:} Developed using React Native, Expo 51, and React Native Web. It utilizes a role-aware bottom tab navigator, modular screen components, linear gradients, vector icons, and asynchronous local caching via AsyncStorage.
    \item \textbf{Application \& API Tier:} Built using Node.js and Express. It serves RESTful endpoints for authentication, regulatory feasibility analysis, architectural design querying, cost computation, and consultation messaging.
    \item \textbf{Data Persistence Tier:} Powered by a relational MySQL 8.0 database utilizing connection pooling (\texttt{mysql2/promise}) to store user accounts, expert profiles, architectural models, cost estimation logs, and consultation message threads.
    \item \textbf{AI Intelligence Layer:} Integrates Google Gemini generative language models (\texttt{@google/genai}) with structured system prompts grounded in BNBC 2020 and local municipal building rules, backed by an internal rule-based heuristic fallback engine.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Feasibility Checker and Building Code Engine}
The Feasibility Checker module automates regulatory compliance verification for land plots in Bangladesh. The user inputs the plot size (in Katha or Sq. Ft.), frontage road width (in Feet), desired number of stories, and the governing municipal authority (RAJUK, CDA, RDA, KDA, or General Pourashava).

The system instantly evaluates:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Permissibility Status:} Whether the requested height is allowable given the frontage road width.
    \item \textbf{Floor Area Ratio (FAR):} Allowable built-up area multiplier in accordance with BNBC 2020 and RAJUK rules.
    \item \textbf{Maximum Ground Coverage (MGC):} Maximum allowable building footprint percentage.
    \item \textbf{Mandatory Setbacks:} Clear minimum setback distances required for front, rear, and side boundaries.
\end{itemize}

\section{Technology Stack}
CivilHub is constructed using a robust, modular, and modern technology stack ensuring high performance, responsive UI rendering, relational data consistency, and seamless AI integration.

\subsection{Architecture Overview}
The system adheres to a decoupled client-server architecture. The cross-platform mobile/web client communicates with the Node.js/Express backend via RESTful JSON endpoints. Persistent application state is managed in MySQL, while AI queries are dispatched to Google Gemini API endpoints.

\subsection{Frontend Technologies}
The frontend is engineered as a unified cross-platform single-page application and mobile client:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{React Native (0.74.5) \& React (18.2.0):} Declarative, component-driven UI library supporting native-like fluid animations and modular layout hierarchies.
    \item \textbf{Expo SDK 51:} Build tooling and runtime framework facilitating rapid multi-platform execution across iOS, Android, and Web browsers (\texttt{react-native-web}).
    \item \textbf{React Navigation (Bottom Tabs 6.5):} Role-aware bottom tab navigation system managing active screen stacks and profile badges.
    \item \textbf{Expo Vector Icons \& Linear Gradient:} Visual design system providing aesthetic iconography (\texttt{Ionicons}, \texttt{MaterialCommunityIcons}) and modern gradient backdrops.
    \item \textbf{AsyncStorage:} Local persistent client-side storage for session tokens, cached filters, and offline fallback states.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Backend Technologies}
The backend service provides a scalable, stateless RESTful API:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Node.js \& Express (4.21):} Event-driven asynchronous JavaScript runtime and web framework managing API routing, request validation, and middleware pipelines.
    \item \textbf{MySQL2 (\texttt{mysql2/promise}):} Asynchronous MySQL connection pooling library handling transactional queries and high-concurrency database operations.
    \item \textbf{JSON Web Tokens (JWT) \& BcryptJS:} Stateless session management, cryptographic token signing, and one-way password hashing.
    \item \textbf{CORS \& Dotenv:} Cross-Origin Resource Sharing handling for web clients and environment variable security management.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{AI and Machine Learning Integration}
CivilHub integrates a specialized AI layer designed for civil engineering and building regulatory advisory:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Google Gemini Generative AI (\texttt{@google/genai}):} Cloud-hosted Large Language Model accessed via RESTful endpoints. The prompt engineering pipeline injects statutory parameters from BNBC 2020, RAJUK setback guidelines, and municipal zoning rules.
    \item \textbf{Custom System Prompting:} Forces structured, safety-first outputs, automatic citation of relevant building code clauses, and mandatory professional review disclaimers.
    \item \textbf{Rule-Based Heuristic Engine Fallback:} An internal regulatory engine (\texttt{bnbcExpertEngine.js}) capable of answering setback, FAR, and soil test queries during offline periods or transient AI outages.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Real-Time Communication and Polling Layer}
To enable responsive, real-time message synchronization between clients and consulting engineers, CivilHub implements a high-efficiency polling mechanism (2.5-second interval) backed by indexed MySQL queries (\texttt{thread\_id}, \texttt{created\_at}). The architecture supports seamless dispatch across isolated threads (\texttt{thread\_client\_architect}, \texttt{thread\_client\_structural}, \texttt{thread\_client\_soil}).

\subsection{Authentication and Security Architecture}
Security is enforced across multiple layers:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Stateless JWT Authorization:} Cryptographically signed tokens with configurable expiration validating user identity on protected routes.
    \item \textbf{Bcrypt Hashing:} Passwords hashed with 10–12 salt rounds before database persistence.
    \item \textbf{Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):} Strict route guards ensuring clients access estimation and browsing suites while engineers access dedicated consultation workspaces.
    \item \textbf{SQL Injection Prevention:} Parameterized prepared statements executed through \texttt{mysql2/promise}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{External Integrations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Google Gemini API:} Generative building code intelligence and reasoning.
    \item \textbf{Municipal Bylaw Knowledge Repositories:} Regulatory datasets encoding rules from BNBC 2020, RAJUK, CDA, RDA, and KDA.
    \item \textbf{Cloud Asset Delivery:} High-resolution architectural elevations and floor plan rendering.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{DevOps and Deployment Architecture}
CivilHub is structured for production deployment:

\section{Challenges}
During the design and implementation of CivilHub, several technical and domain-specific challenges were addressed:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mathematical Modeling of Building Bylaws:} Translating complex, conditional setback tables and FAR multipliers from text-based legal acts into deterministic algorithmic rules across multiple authorities (RAJUK, CDA, RDA, KDA).

    \item \textbf{Grounded AI Prompt Engineering:} Formulating prompt guardrails that prevent LLM hallucinations on critical structural safety parameters and enforce mandatory legal disclaimers.
    \item \textbf{Real-Time Thread Isolation:} Maintaining isolated consultation message queues for different engineering disciplines while supporting rich interactive design card attachments.
\end{itemize}
